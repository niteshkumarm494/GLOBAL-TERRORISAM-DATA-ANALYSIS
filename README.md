# GLOBAL-TERRORISAM-DATA-ANALYSIS

1.Global Trends in Terrorism

Terrorist attacks saw a big jump after 2001, with the highest number happening in 2014. The 1990s were relatively quiet in comparison, but the numbers shot up after 9/11.

2.Most Affected Regions & Countries

Regions Hit Hardest: The Middle East and South Asia, along with Sub-Saharan Africa, have been the most affected. Countries Most Impacted: Iraq, Afghanistan, Pakistan, India, and Colombia have seen the most attacks.

3.Most Common Attack Types

Bombings and explosions, which make up over half of all attacks, are the most frequent method used. Armed assaults are the second most common way attackers strike. Suicide bombings tend to happen mostly in the Middle East.

4.Most Targeted Groups

Civilians, military personnel, police, and government officials are often the targets of these attacks. Important infrastructure, like transportation hubs and marketplaces, also get hit frequently.

5.Most Active Terrorist Groups

The Taliban, ISIS, Al-Qaeda, Boko Haram, and the FARC are the most prominent groups carrying out these attacks.

6.Impact on Human Life & Property

More than 300,000 people have lost their lives in terrorist attacks. Some of the deadliest include 9/11 in 2001, the bombings in Baghdad, and the attacks in Syria.
